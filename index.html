Approach

How I build a growth plan

The same four steps for any online store. The experiments below are what the steps produce once the store type, the channels and the traffic are known.

01Start from the audit

The lab begins once the account audit is done. Its findings set the store type, the channels and the traffic in the diagnostic below, and decide which rounds can run.

02Find the term that moves

Net revenue is the product of five terms. The ad lab works on sessions. This lab works on the other four, one phase each, so every experiment names the term it is meant to move.

03Pick the lever, then build the asset

Every experiment pairs one psychological lever with two emotional triggers and a concrete asset: a product page block, a cart message, an email. The asset follows the lever, so each cell tests a separate idea.

04Test one variable at a time

Each round has one variable, one primary read and net revenue after returns as the guardrail. The traffic answer in the diagnostic decides whether a round runs as an A/B test, a holdout or a before and after read.

Sessions and first order conversion produce new customers. Orders per customer counts the orders that follow, and kept share is the part of revenue that is not refunded. Keep the sale works on two terms: recovered checkouts add to conversion, and fewer avoidable returns raise the kept share. A lift in one term that costs more in another is a loss, which is why every round reports net revenue.

Method

How every round is run

Before round 1Tracking confirmed first

No round starts until the audit confirms that purchases, add to cart and email revenue are tracked correctly. A test read on broken tracking can name a winner that does not exist.

SchedulingOne test per page and audience

Rounds in different channels run at the same time. Two rounds never share a page or an audience, because their results would mix. When the cells of one round sit on different pages, visitors are split once at the start, so each person sees one version only.

Duration and sampleTwo weeks and a planned sample

Every round covers at least two weekly cycles and has a required sample agreed before launch. It closes once each cell reaches that sample, within six weeks. When six weeks cannot reach it, the round runs as a before and after read with its risk written down.

Owned channelsHoldouts for email and SMS

Email and SMS rounds keep a random 10 to 20% of the audience that receives nothing. The lift is measured against no message, which is the comparison that pays for the flow.

ReadingNet revenue decides

A cell that wins on its primary read and loses on net revenue after returns and discounts does not ship. Return rates are read once the return window for the test orders has closed.

Next roundThe winner becomes the control

The winning cell goes live for everyone and becomes the control for the next round in that phase. A tie keeps the current version and moves the round to the next variable.
